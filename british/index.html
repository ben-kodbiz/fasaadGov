Modern British Atrocities Treemap (1962-Present)

Pattern of Atrocities

Since 1962, Britain has been involved in numerous atrocities and war crimes across the globe. This treemap visualizes 18 major incidents spanning over 60 years, with rectangle sizes representing the scale and ongoing impact of each atrocity. The visualization reveals a consistent pattern of:

  • Colonial Crimes: Forced displacement and ethnic cleansing (Chagos Islands)
  • Military Operations: War crimes in Iraq, Afghanistan, and Northern Ireland
  • Covert Operations: Secret wars and support for brutal regimes
  • Systematic Cover-ups: Operation Legacy and destruction of evidence
  • Current Complicity: Ongoing support for atrocities in Gaza, Yemen, and Sudan

Key Modern Atrocities

Chagos Islands Displacement (1965-1973)

Forced removal of 2,000 Chagossians to make way for US military base, ongoing legal battles for right of return.

Operation Legacy (1950s-1970s)

Systematic destruction of documents detailing colonial crimes across 37 countries to hide British atrocities.

Bloody Sunday Massacre (1972)

British paratroopers killed 14 unarmed civilians during civil rights march in Northern Ireland.

Gaza Complicity (2023-present)

UK arms exports and support enabling Israeli war crimes and potential genocide in Gaza.

Ongoing Atrocities

Britain continues to be complicit in atrocities worldwide through:

  • Arms Sales: Weapons exports to regimes committing war crimes (Saudi Arabia, Israel)
  • Military Support: Training and intelligence for forces targeting civilians
  • Diplomatic Protection: Using UN Security Council position to shield allies from accountability
  • Financial Complicity: London as hub for money laundering from conflict zones

Lack of Accountability

Despite extensive evidence of war crimes, Britain has consistently avoided accountability through:

  • Legal Immunity: Overseas Operations Act limiting prosecutions of military personnel
  • Cover-ups: Closure of investigations like IHAT without prosecutions
  • Document Destruction: Systematic destruction of evidence (Operation Legacy)
  • Political Protection: Government refusal to cooperate with international investigations
