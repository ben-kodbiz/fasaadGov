UAE Atrocities Analysis Overview

Scale of UAE Regional Interventions

This treemap documents alleged atrocities committed by the UAE across 4 major regions since 2010, representing a pattern of military interventions, proxy support, and human rights violations. The visualization shows:

  • Ongoing Conflicts: Active involvement in Sudan, Yemen, and Libya with documented war crimes
  • Arms Embargo Violations: Systematic breaches of UN arms embargoes across multiple conflicts
  • Proxy Warfare: Use of mercenaries and local militias to advance UAE interests
  • Humanitarian Impact: Millions displaced, hundreds of thousands killed through UAE-backed operations

Major Atrocities by Region

Sudan (2019-Present)

Backing RSF paramilitary forces enabling genocide in Darfur, ethnic cleansing of Masalit people, arms supply violations, and famine as weapon of war. Over 150,000 killed, 12 million displaced.

Yemen (2015-Present)

Saudi-led coalition participation with torture centers, mercenary recruitment, civilian bombings, and humanitarian blockades contributing to world's worst humanitarian crisis.

Libya (2014-Present)

Support for General Haftar through arms supply, drone strikes on civilians, mercenary deployment, and involvement in mass graves and ethnic violence.

Other African Countries

Military bases in Somalia/Eritrea, Wagner Group funding in CAR, migrant worker abuses in UAE, and neo-colonial resource extraction across the continent.

Current Ongoing Atrocities (2023-Present)

The UAE continues active involvement in multiple conflicts with documented human rights violations:

  • Sudan: ICJ case filed in 2025 for genocide complicity through RSF backing and arms supply
  • Yemen: Continued coalition support despite official troop withdrawal claims
  • Libya: Ongoing arms embargo violations and mercenary operations
  • Migrant Abuses: Systematic detention and deportation of African workers within UAE

Patterns of UAE Operations

Analysis reveals consistent patterns in UAE foreign interventions:

  • Proxy Warfare: Using local militias and mercenaries to avoid direct accountability
  • Arms Embargo Violations: Systematic breaches of UN restrictions across multiple conflicts
  • Economic Motivations: Resource extraction (gold, ports) driving military interventions
  • Denial Strategy: Consistent rejection of allegations while positioning as mediator
  • Humanitarian Cover: Using aid operations to disguise military supply lines

Documentation Sources

This analysis is compiled from credible sources including:

  • Human Rights Organizations: Amnesty International, Human Rights Watch investigations
  • United Nations: UN reports, arms embargo monitoring, fact-finding missions
  • International Courts: ICJ filings, international tribunal evidence
  • Investigative Journalism: Guardian, leaked UN reports, eyewitness testimonies
  • Academic Research: Think tank analyses and regional security studies

UAE Official Responses

The UAE consistently denies direct involvement in atrocities across all regions:

  • Sudan: Claims to provide only humanitarian aid, calls ICJ case a "publicity stunt"
  • Yemen: Emphasizes counter-terrorism focus and development projects
  • Libya: Positions support as anti-Islamist stability efforts
  • Africa: Highlights economic partnerships and security cooperation with 24+ nations

🌍 Detailed Regional Analysis

🇸🇩 Sudan: The Genocide Enabler (2019-Present)

Context: Sudan's civil war began in 2019 between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF). The UAE has been accused of backing the RSF, enabling massive atrocities.

Key Atrocities:
  • Arms Supply Violations: UAE flights delivering Chinese drones and missiles via Chad and Uganda, violating UN arms embargoes
  • Zamzam Camp Massacre (Aug 2025): Thousands slaughtered in ethnic-targeted violence against Masalit people
  • Famine as Weapon: Hospital bombings and starvation tactics enabled by UAE-backed mercenaries
Impact: 150,000+ killed, 12 million displaced, famine affecting millions. Sudan filed ICJ case in March 2025 accusing UAE of genocide complicity.
UAE Response: Denies arming RSF, claims humanitarian aid only, calls ICJ case "publicity stunt"

🇾🇪 Yemen: The Forgotten War Crimes (2015-Present)

Context: UAE joined Saudi-led coalition in Yemen's civil war, deploying troops and establishing detention facilities across southern Yemen.

Key Atrocities:
  • Torture Centers: UAE-run detention facilities with systematic torture, sexual violence, and forced disappearances
  • Mercenary Recruitment: Up to 4,000 Sudanese fighters recruited for proxy warfare
  • Humanitarian Blockade: Naval blockades restricting aid, contributing to world's worst humanitarian crisis
Impact: Hundreds of thousands killed, millions facing famine, infrastructure destroyed, regional destabilization
UAE Response: Claims troop withdrawal in 2019, emphasizes counter-terrorism and development focus

🇱🇾 Libya: The Arms Embargo Violator (2014-Present)

Context: UAE backed General Khalifa Haftar's Libyan National Army against the UN-recognized Government of National Accord.

Key Atrocities:
  • Arms Supply: Provided drones, missiles, and US-origin weapons enabling civilian strikes
  • Mercenary Operations: Deployed Sudanese and other African mercenaries leading to summary executions
  • Mass Graves: Involvement in ethnic violence and systematic killings
Impact: Prolonged civil war, mass displacement, destruction of state institutions, regional instability
UAE Response: Denies embargo violations, positions support as anti-Islamist stability efforts

🌍 Other African Countries: Neo-Colonial Expansion

Context: UAE's Africa strategy intensified post-2015, involving military bases, resource extraction, and proxy operations across the continent.

Key Operations:
  • Somalia/Eritrea: Military bases fueling separatism and Red Sea tensions
  • Central African Republic: Wagner Group funding enabling gold mining atrocities
  • Migrant Abuses: Detention and torture of 800+ African workers in UAE
  • Resource Extraction: Port investments displacing locals, fueling conflicts
Impact: Regional destabilization, human rights violations, economic exploitation, forced displacement
UAE Response: Emphasizes development partnerships with 24+ African nations, denies destabilization
